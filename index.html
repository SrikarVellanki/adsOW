06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get rolling.

Can I book a campaign right now?+

adsOW is in the concept and pre-pilot stage, so campaigns are not yet confirmed as available. Contact us to express interest and discuss your requirements.

What campaign durations are planned?+

The proposed model is built around 7, 15, and 30-day campaign slots. Availability and final terms will be confirmed after the pilot model is validated.

Which areas will you cover?+

Bengaluru is the planned first-pilot market. Specific zones and coverage have not yet been confirmed.

How do I enquire about advertising?+

Email us with your business name, preferred area, campaign duration, and a short description of your goals. We'll use that information to understand your interest.
